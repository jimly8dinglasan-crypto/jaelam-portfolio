BACKGROUND

My path so far.

I’m Jaelam Ryle G. Dinglasan, a third-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at Negros Oriental State University. My education has introduced me to a broad range of computing topics, and I’m continuing to build confidence through practice.

I’m especially interested in computer hardware and troubleshooting: understanding what is happening under the surface, then working through a problem methodically. I also enjoy learning how simple, well-made websites can make information easier to find.

This portfolio is a record of my current learning, not a claim to expertise. I want to keep improving my technical foundations, communicate clearly, and make useful things that work for real people.

What I’m working toward

Develop dependable IT fundamentals, gain hands-on experience, and build a body of small projects I can explain and improve.
